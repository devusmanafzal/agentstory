INDUSTRIAL AI WORKBENCH
FACILITATOR GUIDE

Purpose

Use this package to guide customers through synthetic industrial scenarios and explore how Microsoft Copilot experiences can work with a connected Knowledge Pack.

Before the demo

1. Read DISCLAIMER.txt.
2. Confirm that participants understand that all content is synthetic.
3. Choose a use-case and download its Knowledge Pack.
4. Decide whether participants will attach files directly or use OneDrive or SharePoint.
5. Do not use confidential, personal, regulated, or otherwise sensitive customer information.

During the demo

1. Start from Start-Here.html.
2. Open the selected use-case.
3. Use the recommended Copilot experience shown on the page.
4. Follow the Knowledge Pack and Prompt Pack guidance.
5. Compare the generated outputs with the expected outcomes.
6. Discuss source grounding, uncertainty, contradictions, and actionability.
