The story behind the Knowledge Pack

How a quality signal became a decision

On 8 January, the plant quality team records the first battery-management warning during end-of-line testing. It is small enough to be treated as a signal to watch, not yet a production crisis.

Over the following weeks, the signal keeps growing. Email messages and quality workbooks show the fault rate moving above the plant baseline and appearing in several production lots.

A supplier firmware change appears to align with the timing, but engineering sees another possible contribution in retry timing. The supplier and engineering teams do not initially agree on what the evidence proves.

Plant leadership now has to decide with incomplete information. The team contains the affected lots, starts controlled validation, and prepares a recommendation and leadership presentation for the next review.
